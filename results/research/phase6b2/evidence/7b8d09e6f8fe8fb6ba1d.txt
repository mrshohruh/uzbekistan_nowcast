When calculating in US dollars at the average annual exchange rate of the Central 
Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan, GDP in 2019 amounted to 57 907 million USD (in 
2018 - 50 397 million USD).  
For information: the official US dollar rate to the soum in average annual terms amounted 
to: in 2018 - 8 069 soums, in 2019 - 8 839 soums. 
GDP per capita of the country amounted to 15  242.0 thousand soums (or the 
equivalent of 1 724 USD) and, compared with 2018, increased in real terms by 3.6%. 
GDP growth per capita in 2017 and 2018 was 2.7% and 3.6%, respectively. 
 
GDP – one of the most important indicators of the System of National Accounts, 
characterizing the final result of the production activities of resident economic units, which 
is measured by the value of goods and services produced by these units for final use. 
The volume index (real growth rate) of GDP is calculated as the ratio of GDP of 
the reporting period, calculated at constant prices, i.e. at prices of the correspon ding 
period of last year (real GDP), to GDP at current prices of the corresponding period of last 
year. 
The GDP deflator index characterizes the average price change in the economy 
and is calculated as the ratio of nominal GDP (GDP of the reporting period in current 
prices) to real GDP.

sectors of the economy. Gross value added generated by all sectors of the economy

to 9 .1 % and showed a similar increase of 5.6 % (contribution to GDP growth -0.6 
percentage points).

 At the end of 2019, agriculture, forestry and fisheries showed a positive 
growth rate of 2.5 %. The contribution of this industry to GDP growth was 0.7 
percentage points. the Positive dynamics in agriculture, forestry and fisheries is 
associated with an increase in crop production by 3.7% and livestock production 
by 1.7 %. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
In the industry, there is an increase in value added by 6.6%. At the same time, 
the contribution to GDP growth from industrial production amounted to 1.6 percentage 
points. The positive dynamics in this industry was ensured mainly due to an increase in 
the value added of the manufacturing industry by 9.4% (in 2018 - by 7.9%, 2017 - by 
4.2%). The added value of the mining industry and quarrying showed a decrease in 
comparable prices by 1.0% after a noticeable increase in the previous two years (growth 
in 2018 - by 26.5%, 2017 - by 17.6%).

In comparison with 2018, construction work increased by 19.0 % as a result of 
large-scale construction of residential complexes, construction and major reconstruction 
of social and engineering infrastructure facilities, as well as during the implementation 
of investment projects for the construction and modernization of enterprises in basic 
industries. The positive contribution to GDP growth from the construction industry was 
1.0 percentage points. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
At the end of 2019, the gross value added of the services sector amounted to 
165 053.2 billion soums, and compared with 2018, it increased by 5.1%, which led to 
a GDP growth of 1.7 percentage points. Including trade services (including vehicle 
repairs) inc reased by 4.9%, accommodation and catering services - by 5.9%, 
transportation and storage - by 4.9%, information and communication - by 8.0% and 
other services industries - by 5.0%. 
In 2019, the largest share in the structure of gross value added of trade 
(including repair of motor vehicles) was in retail trade (excluding trade in motor 
vehicles) and amounted to 67.3% (in 2018 - 67.3%, 2017 - 68.2% ) The share of 
wholesale trade (except for trade in motor vehicles) reached 26.0% (in 2018 - 26.2%, 
2017 - 26.5%), wholesale and retail trade in motor vehicles and their repair - 6.7% (in 
2018 year - 6.5%, 2017 - 5.3%). 
 
For information: 
According to preliminary data, the volume of construction work performed by large 
construction organizations in 2019 amounted to 16  904.6 billion soums  (108.5% 
compared to 2018, the share in the total volume of construction work amounted to 24.6%). 
Along with this, the volume of construction work carried out by small enterprises and 
microfirms reached 37 593.5 billion soums (129.5% of the level of 2018, the share in the 
total volume of construction work - 54.6%). The volume of construction work carried out 
by individuals amounted to 14 356.3 billion soums (108.3% of the level of 2018, the share 
in the total volume of construction work - 20.8%).

In 2019, the GDP deflator index was 119.2% relative to prices of the same period 
in 2018. The highest values of deflator indices in the structure of GDP were noted in 
industry - 136.9% (in particular in mining and quarrying - 151.4%; manufacturing - 
132.7%; other industries - 140.2%) and other services sectors - 128.1%. 
Deflator indices are lower than in the economy as a whole (119.2%) were 
observed in agriculture, forestry and fisheries - 112.1%, construction - 113.7%, trade, 
accommodation and catering services - 114.7 %, transportation and storage, 
information and communications - 110.8% and in net taxes on products - 96.6%. 
 
For information: 
In 2019, 92.9 thousand new small enterprises and microfirms were created (excluding 
dekhkan and farm enterprises), which is 1.9 times more than in 2018. The largest number of 
small enterprises and micro-firms were created in the field of trade (39.1%), industry (21.0%), 
construction (9.4%), agriculture, forestry and fisheries (8.0%), and the field of accommodation 
services and food (7.8%), transportation and storage (3.2%).
