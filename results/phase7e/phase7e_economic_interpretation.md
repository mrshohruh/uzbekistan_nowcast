# Phase 7E economic interpretation

Factors measure statistical co-movement. Bridge coefficient products are predictive GDP attribution in percentage points; they are not causal effects, structural multipliers, official sector shares or national-accounting contributions. The intercept and persistence term explain much of the forecast level; centered factor terms explain deviations relative to their bridge training means.
