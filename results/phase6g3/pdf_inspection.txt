 GDP – is one of the most important 
indicators of the System of National 
Accounts, characterizing the final result  
of the production activity of resident 
economic units, which is measured by the 
value of goods and services produced  
by these units for final use.  
The index of physical volume (real 
growth rates) of GDP is calculated  as 
the ratio of the GDP of the reporting  
period, calculated at constant  prices, i.e. 
in prices of the corresponding  period  
of the previous  year (real GDP) to GDP 
in current prices of the corresponding  
period of the previous year.  
 
 
 The GDP deflator index 
characterizes the average change  
in prices in the economy and  
is calculated as the ratio of nominal 
GDP (GDP of the reporting period  
at current prices) to real GDP.

GDP is one of the most important indicators of the System of National Accounts,
which characterizes the final result of the production activity of resident economic units,
which is measured by the value of goods and services produced by these units for final use.
The physical volume index (real growth rates) of GDP is calculated as 
the ratio of the GDP of the reporting period, calculated in constant prices, i.e. at 
prices of the corresponding period of the last year (real GDP) to GDP at current 
prices of the corresponding period of the last year.
The GDP deflator index characterizes the average change in 
prices in the economy and is calculated as the ratio of nominal GDP (GDP of the 
reporting period in current prices) to real GDP.
As the chart above illustrates, in January -September 2020, GDP growth 
slowed to 0.4% versus 5.9% in January -September 2019 and 5.7% in January -
September 2018. At the same time, a slowdown in the GDP deflator index to 111.8% 
was noted after 118.5% in January -September 2019 and 130.5% in January -
September 2018. 
 
For reference: in January-September 2020, the official exchange rate of 
the US dollar in relation to the soum averaged  9 940.9 soums, in January -
September 2019 – 8 621.4 soums. 
  
For reference: GDP per capita is determined by dividing its total volume at 
current prices by the average population of the country for a certain period of time. The 
average resident population of the Republic of Uzbekistan was: in January-September 
2018 - 32 871 thousand people, January-September 2019 - 33 490 thousand people, 
January-September 2020 - 34 144 thousand people.
