  In accordance with the preliminary assessment, in January-September 
2023, the GDP of the Republic of Uzbekistan in current prices amounted to  
750 925.7 billion soums and, compared to January-September 2022, increased in 
real terms by 5.8%. The GDP deflator index, in relation to prices in January-
September 2022, amounted to 111.2%.  
  Gross domestic product (GDP) is one of the most important indicators of 
the System of National Accounts (SNA), characterizing the final result of the 
production activities of resident economic units, which is measured by the value of 
goods and services produced by these units for final use.

Gross added value of agriculture, forestry and fisheries  
for January-September 2019-2023 
According to the results of January-September 2023, agriculture, forestry 
and fisheries showed positive growth rates of 4.1% (in January-September 2022 
– 3.5%, January-September 2021 – 4.0%, January-September 2020 – 2.6%,

farming by 3.3% (in January-September 2022 – 3.8%, January-September 2021 - 
2.6%, January-September 2020 – 2.2%, January-September 2019 – 2.6%).  
At the same time, crop production increased by 4.9% over the specified 
period (in January-September 2022 – 3.3%, January-September 2021 – 5.7%, 
January-September 2020 – 3.1%, January-September 2019 – 2.3%).

Gross added value of ICT services for January-September 2021-2023 
In January-September 2023, the share of information and communication technology 
(ICT) services in the country’s economy was 1.9%. 
The added value of ICT services consists of communication services – 59.2%, computer 
programming, consultations and other related services – 22.9%, data hosting and processing 
services, Web portals – 10.3%, repair of computers and communication equipment – 3.9%, 
software releases – 3.7%. 
Gross added value created in small entrepreneurship (business)  
by type of economic activity for January-September 2023 
         Based on the results of January-September 
2023, small enterprises (businesses) created 
added value in the amount of 363 965.3 billion

economy. At the same time, the share of 
agriculture, forestry and fisheries in the total GVA 
of small enterprises (businesses) was 44.6%, 
industry – 10.9%, construction – 9.6% and the 
service sector – 35.0%. 
         The share of small 
entrepreneurship (business) in the main 
sectors of the economy was: in 
agriculture, forestry and fishing – 96.5%, 
construction – 76.0%, services – 40.5% 
and industry – 21.7%.
