Gross domestic product (GDP)  
is one of the most important indicators 
of the System of National Accounts 
(SNA), characterizing the final result  
of the production activity of resident 
economic units, which is measured  
by the value of goods and services 
produced by these units for final use. 
         The index of physical volume (real 
growth rate) of GDP is calculated  
as the ratio of the GDP of the reporting 
period, calculated in constant prices, i.e. 
in the prices of the corresponding period 
of the previous year (real GDP) to the GDP 
in current prices of the corresponding 
period of the previous year.

   Non-observed economy of the Republic of Uzbekistan  
   for January-September 2024 
      According to the results of January-September 2024, the gross added value  
of the informal and shadow economy in the Republic of Uzbekistan amounted  
to 357 507.6 billion soums, and its share in the gross domestic product was 35.2 %. 
      Of these, the gross added value of the informal economy in the Republic  
of Uzbekistan amounted to 274 028.6 billion soums, and its share in the gross 
domestic product was 27.0.%. The gross added value of the shadow economy 
amounted to 83 479.1 billion soums, and its share in the gross domestic product  
was 8.2 %. 
Non-observed economy – types of economic activity that are not fully or partially 
covered by regular statistical observations and are also estimated using indirect methods 
when reflected in statistical indicators. 
The informal economy is an economic activity related to the production of goods 
(provision of services) carried out by households or individuals without registration  
in the established manner. 
The shadow economy is an economic activity that is not prohibited by law,  
but is intentionally hidden from government authorities for the purpose of tax evasion and 
non-compliance with legal requirements.
