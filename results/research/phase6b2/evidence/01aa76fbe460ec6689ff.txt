PRODUCTION OF GROSS 
DOMESTIC PRODUCT  
 GDP – is one of the most important 
indicators of the System of National 
Accounts, characterizing the final result  
of the production activity of resident 
economic units, which is measured by the 
value of goods and services produced  
by these units for final use.  
The index of physical volume (real 
growth rates) of GDP is calculated  as 
the ratio of the GDP of the reporting  
period, calculated at constant  prices, i.e. 
in prices of the corresponding  period  
of the previous  year (real GDP) to GDP 
in current prices of the corresponding  
period of the previous year.  
 
 
 The GDP deflator index 
characterizes the average change  
in prices in the economy and  
is calculated as the ratio of nominal 
GDP (GDP of the reporting period  
at current prices) to real GDP.
