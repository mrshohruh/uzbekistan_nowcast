     The GDP deflator index characterizes the 
average price change in the economy and is 
calculated as the ratio of nominal GDP (GDP of 
the reporting period in current prices) to real 
GDP. 
 
 
 
 
 GDP is one of the most important indicators  
of the System of National Accounts, which 
characterizes the final result of the production 
activity of resident economic units, which  
is measured by the value of goods and services 
produced by these units for final use. 
 The volume index (real growth rates) of GDP is 
calculated as the ratio of the GDP of the 
reporting period, calculated in constant prices, 
i.e. in prices of the corresponding period of the 
last year (real GDP) to GDP in current prices  
of the corresponding period of the last year.
