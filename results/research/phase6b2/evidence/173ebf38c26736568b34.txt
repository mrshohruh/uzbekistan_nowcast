I. Retail trade turnover 
As of September 1, 2026, the number of commercial enterprises operating in the retail 
trade sector amounted to 92,434. Of these, the number of large enterprises was 428, and the 
number of small enterprises and micro-firms was 92,006. 
According to the results of January-August 2026, in Uzbekistan retail trade turnover 
reached 370,557.1 billion soums, increasing by 18.8 percent in real terms compared to the 
corresponding period of 2025. The turnover of large enterprises amounted to 73,824.8 billion 
soums ( a real growth rate  of 120.0 %), while that of small business  entities amounted  to 
296,732.2 billion soums (a real growth rate of 118.5 %).

IV.  Methodological notes 
When forming statistical indicators of wholesale and retail trade turnover, regulatory legal 
acts of the Republic of Uzbekistan regulating the activities of wholesale and retail trade 
enterprises and organizations, as well as international manuals and recommendations such as 
"System of National Accounts 2008 (SNA -2008)," "International Recommendations on Retail and 
Wholesale Trade Statistics" (UN, 2008), "Methodological recommendations for statistical 
observation of retail trade turnover and calculation of its physical volume index" (Statistical 
Committee of the CIS, 2011) were used. 
Statistics of wholesale and retail trade turnover are calculated taking into account the 
results of the statistical assessment of the informal and non-observed economy. 
In some cases, small differences between the final value and the sum of the addends are 
explained by the rounding of the data.
