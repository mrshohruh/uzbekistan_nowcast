In accordance with the Program of State Statistical Works, Goskomstat 
made a preliminary estimate of gross domestic product (hereinafter - GDP) for 
January-March 2018. 
According to preliminary data, the GDP of the Republic of Uzbekistan in 
January-March 2018 i n current prices amounted to 64 958,0 billion soums and 
increased by 5,1 % compared to the corresponding period of the previous year. 
The index - the GDP deflator relative to the prices in January -March 2017 
amounted to 137,4 %. 
GDP per capita amounted to 1 986 thousand UZS, which is 3,3 % more 
than in the corresponding period last year.
