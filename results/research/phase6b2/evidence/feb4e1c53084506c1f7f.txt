 Gross value added of agriculture, forestry and fisheries, January - March 
Based on the results of January-March 2024 agriculture, forestry and 
fisheries showed a positive growth rate of 3.6% (in January-March 2023 - 3.1%, 
January-March 2022 - 2.7%, January-March 2021 – 3.0%, January-March 2020 – 
3.1%).     
The positive dynamics in this industry is due to the growth of livestock 
farming by 3.6% (in January-March 2023 - 3.6%, January-March 2022 - 2.4%, 
January-March 2021 - 2.9%, January-March 2020 – 3.3%).  
Along with this, crop production during the specified period increased  
by 4.4% (in January-March 2023 - a decrease of 2.3%, in January-March 2022 - 
an increase of 8.5%, in January-March 2021 - growth by 5.0%, January-March 
2020 – growth by 2.5%).
