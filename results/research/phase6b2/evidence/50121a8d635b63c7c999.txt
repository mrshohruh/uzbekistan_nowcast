UZS and, compared with January-June 2021, increased in real terms by 5.4%. Slight changes were noted in the sectoral structure of GDP. Thus, the share of industry in the sectoral structure of GDP (GVA) increased from 28.6% to 29.2%, construction - from 7.1% to 7.2%, services - from 41.5% to 42.2%, while the share of agriculture, forestry and fisheries decreased from 22.8% to 21.4%.
