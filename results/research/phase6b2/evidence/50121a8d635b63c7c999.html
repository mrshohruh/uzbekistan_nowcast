What is the structure of GDP by sectors of the economy in January-June 2022?

26 July 2022

According to the results of January-June 2022, the gross domestic product (GDP) of the Republic of Uzbekistan at current prices amounted to 389.6 trillion. UZS and, compared with January-June 2021, increased in real terms by 5.4%.

Slight changes were noted in the sectoral structure of GDP. Thus, the share of industry in the sectoral structure of GDP (GVA) increased from 28.6% to 29.2%, construction - from 7.1% to 7.2%, services - from 41.5% to 42.2%, while the share of agriculture, forestry and fisheries decreased from 22.8% to 21.4%.
