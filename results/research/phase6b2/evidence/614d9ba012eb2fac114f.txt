In accordance with the preliminary assessment, in January-June 2024,  
the GDP of the Republic of Uzbekistan in current prices amounted to 567 364.3 
billion soums and, compared to January-June 2023, increased in real terms   
by 6.4 %. The GDP deflator index, in relation to prices in January-June 2023, 
amounted to 112.5 %.

one of the most important indicators of 
the System of National Accounts (SNA), 
characterizing the final result of the 
production activities of resident 
economic units, which is measured by 
the cost of goods and services produced 
by these units for final use.
