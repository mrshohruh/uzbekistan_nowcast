GROSS DOMESTIC PRODUCT 
 
According to preliminary estimates, the GDP of the Republic of Uzbekistan 
for January - March 2019 in current prices amounted to 91,097.7 billion soums 
and, in comparison with January - March 2018, in real terms increased by 5.3%. 
The GDP deflator relative to January-March 2018 prices amounted to 120.4%. 
GDP per capita amounted to 2 734.4 thousand soums, which was 3.4% 
more than in the corresponding period of 2018.

The economic growth rate is due to the positive dynamics in the main 
sectors of the economy. Gross value added (hereinafter - GVA), created by all 
sectors of the economy, acco unted for 88.7% of total GDP and increased by 
5.2% (contribution to GDP growth - 4.5 percentage points). Net taxes on 
products amounted to 11.3% in the structure of GDP and showed an increase of 
5.8% (contribution to GDP growth - 0.8 percentage points).

and storage - by 4.6%, information and communication - by 2.7% and other 
sectors of services - by 5.7%.

from the same period of 2018. The highest values of deflator in the structure of

forestry and fisheries - 114.8%, construction - 113.9%, trade, accommodation 
and food services - 112.3%, transportation and storage, information and
